## Goethe-Zertifikat Exam Dates 2026 in Germany (A1–C2)

Goethe-Zertifikat exam dates in Germany are set separately by each Prüfungszentrum. There is no single nationwide 2026 timetable. Find your centre first, then confirm the level, module, registration deadline and available places on its official page.

Plan your prep backwards from the Prüfungstermin starting today: [practice the Schreiben section](https://goethecoach.de/en/b1-writing.html) with instant AI feedback on GoetheCoach.

### Where can you find Goethe-Zertifikat exam dates for 2026?

Dates vary by city, level and module. Use the Goethe-Institut’s official exam-centre finder, then open the schedule published by your chosen centre.

- [Goethe-Institut: Prüfungsorte in Deutschland](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/prf/ort.html)
- [Goethe-Institut: Prüfungsergebnisse und Bestehensregeln](https://www.goethe.de/de/spr/prf/pes.html)

### How to choose the right exam date

Choose your level and Prüfungszentrum first. Then check whether the exam is offered as a full exam or in modules, when registration closes, and whether places remain. Plan backwards only from a confirmed date because schedules and deadlines vary.

### Preparation timeline (week-by-week countdown)

The most effective plan counts backwards from the Prüfungstermin. This countdown works for every level from A1 to C2.

| Time before the exam | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Week 12–8 | Build the base: Wortschatz and Grammatik for the exam topics; learn the exam format. |
| Week 8–4 | Practise all four Module deliberately; write your first full Schreiben task (Forumsbeitrag, Brief or E-Mail). |
| Week 4–2 | Two complete timed mock exams; make Redemittel and Konnektoren automatic. |
| Week 2–1 | Drill your weakest Modul; rehearse Sprechen out loud; check Anmeldung and documents. |
| Final week | Revise, don't learn new things; sleep early; confirm the venue and travel. |

### Best tips to prepare

Practise each Modul with the real format, not just vocabulary lists. For **Schreiben**, write complete texts (Forumsbeitrag, Brief, E-Mail) and have them scored — memorise fixed Redemittel and Konnektoren so you can structure fast under exam pressure. For **Sprechen**, rehearse out loud and on the clock. For **Lesen** and **Hören**, train skimming and spotting key words. Simulate at least two complete exams under real conditions so the timing does not surprise you on exam day.

### The week before and exam day

In the final week, revise only what you already know and sleep enough. Check your Anmeldung, the exact location of the Prüfungszentrum, and how you will get there. On exam day, bring a valid photo ID and your registration confirmation, and arrive at least 20–30 minutes early. Phone off, water with you — and between the Module, take a breath rather than flipping back.

### Key Takeaways

- There is **no single nationwide exam calendar** covering every centre.
- Exam dates and registration deadlines vary by city, level and module.
- Confirm the date, fee and available places directly with the Prüfungszentrum.
- Ideally, start preparing **8–12 weeks** before your confirmed date.
- For modular exams, the **pass mark is 60% for each module**.
- Write real exam tasks with feedback and complete at least two timed mock exams.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Where can I find Goethe-Zertifikat exam dates for 2026 in Germany?**

A: Use the Goethe-Institut’s official exam-centre finder and open the schedule for your chosen Prüfungszentrum. Each centre publishes its own dates.

**Q: What is the registration deadline?**

A: It varies by Prüfungszentrum, level and sitting. Check the deadline directly with the centre and register early because places are limited.

**Q: Is eight weeks enough for the Goethe-Zertifikat B2?**

A: If you already have a solid B1–B2 level, eight weeks of focused practice may be enough. Otherwise, allow more time.

**Q: Can I retake individual modules?**

A: Individual modules can generally be retaken in modular exams. The Prüfungszentrum sets the available dates and detailed conditions.

**Q: What should I bring on exam day?**

A: Follow your centre’s invitation. You will usually need valid official photo identification and your registration confirmation.

**Q: How should I start preparing?**

A: Confirm your level, centre and date first. Then plan backwards and begin early with timed writing tasks.
