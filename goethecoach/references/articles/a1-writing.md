### What Is Tested in the A1 Writing Exam?

The Goethe-Zertifikat A1 writing module consists of two tasks completed together with the reading section in 45 minutes. You demonstrate your ability to put basic German into writing.

### Assessment Criteria in A1 Writing

- Task Fulfilment: Were all given content points addressed?
- Comprehensibility: Can the message be understood even if there are some errors?
- Vocabulary: Are basic everyday words (family, home, work, numbers, dates) used correctly?
- Grammar: Are basic structures visible — verb conjugation, articles, negation?
- Format: Was an appropriate greeting and sign-off used in the email?

### Tips for the A1 Writing Exam

- Address every point. The most common A1 mistake is forgetting one of the three content points. Read the task carefully!
- Short, clear sentences are perfectly fine. Nobody expects complex grammar at A1. Clear, short sentences are better than long, error-filled ones.
- Learn the basic email template. Greeting (Liebe/r …, Hallo …), body text (3 points), sign-off (Viele Grüße, Dein/e …).
- Practise with a timer. The writing exam is only 20 minutes. Simulate real exam pressure — GoetheCoach does exactly that.
- Proofread your text. Check articles (der, die, das), verb endings, and whether you have included all required information.

### Why Does the Goethe-Zertifikat A1 Matter?

The Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1) is particularly important for family reunification in Germany. Anyone bringing a spouse or children to Germany often needs to prove basic German language knowledge — the A1 certificate is officially recognised by German embassies for this purpose.
