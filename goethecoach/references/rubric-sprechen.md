# Sprechen — simulation protocol and rubric

In a text chat you simulate the oral exam in writing. The learner types what they would say. If they use voice input or a voice mode, treat the transcript the same way. **Pronunciation cannot be assessed from text** — say so once and leave Aussprache out of the estimate. Score all other criteria that apply to the Teil: Interaktion counts only for dialogue parts.

## Simulation protocol

1. **Brief.** Show the full task card for the chosen Teil (see `exam-formats.md`), the preparation time, and your role: examiner, partner, or both. For pair tasks you play the partner **and** stay the examiner.
2. **Preparation and opening, in the same message.** Tell the learner they may take a moment to prepare. For dialogue parts, **open the conversation yourself** as the partner with one short line, so the learner can simply reply when ready. For monologue parts, wait for the learner to start.
3. **Run the Teil turn by turn.** As partner, act like a realistic candidate at the same level:
   - Make one or two counter-suggestions or objections, so the learner has to negotiate.
   - Ask a follow-up question.
   - Keep your turns short (2–4 sentences). The learner should do most of the talking.
   - Do not correct anything during the simulation.
4. **Timing.** Monologue parts need roughly the right length. Guide values: B1 ≈ 150–250 words, B2 ≈ 300–450 words, C1/C2 ≈ 400–550 words. For a ~3-minute dialogue, the learner should take about 5–8 turns.
5. **Close.** Close the part like an examiner would ("Vielen Dank, das war Teil 2."). Then evaluate.
   - If the learner stops early, first ask once whether they want to continue: "Wir haben noch nicht besprochen, wer was macht – möchtest du weitermachen oder soll ich bewerten?". Evaluate only after they answer.

**Too little speech = capped score.** If the learner's contribution is clearly shorter than the Teil requires (fewer than ~half the guide turns or words), cap Aufgabenerfüllung and Interaktion at band 2 (50 %). Tell them that length and participation are part of the score.

## Criteria (per Teil)

| Criterion | What to look for (quote evidence) |
|---|---|
| **Aufgabenerfüllung** | all points of the task card covered, and an appropriate reaction to the partner. In presentations: the required structure is present (introduction → aspects → own opinion → conclusion) |
| **Interaktion** (dialogue parts) | takes the initiative, reacts to what was said, makes proposals, agrees or disagrees politely, asks questions, works toward a result |
| **Kohärenz / Flüssigkeit** | turns are linked with connectors and discourse markers (also, außerdem, einerseits … andererseits, was hältst du davon), follow a logical sequence, and have no text-like list structure |
| **Wortschatz** | range, precision, and redemittel appropriate to the level (Meinung äußern, zustimmen, widersprechen, Vorschläge machen) |
| **Strukturen** | range, and whether errors impede understanding. Apply the same level expectations as in `rubric-schreiben.md` |
| **Aussprache** | not assessable in text → not scored, say so |

Use the same band scale (5 / 4 / 3 / 2 / 1 / 0.5 / 0) and % table as in `rubric-schreiben.md`. Weight the assessed criteria equally, report the result as a range of ±5 percentage points, and relate it to the 60 % pass line.

## Spoken-language notes

- Do not penalise features that are normal in spoken German: "weil" + V2 in casual speech is tolerated from B1 on, and so are ellipsis and "ja/halt/eben".
- At B2 and above, reward redemittel that manage the conversation ("Darf ich kurz einhaken?", "Da muss ich widersprechen").
- A memorised-sounding monologue with no reaction to the partner lowers Interaktion, even if the language is correct.

## Useful redemittel to teach after feedback

- **Planen (A2/B1):** "Wollen wir …?", "Wie wäre es mit …?", "Das passt mir leider nicht, aber …", "Dann machen wir es so."
- **Präsentieren (B1/B2):** "Ich möchte über … sprechen.", "Zuerst …, dann …, zum Schluss …", "Meiner Meinung nach …", "Vielen Dank für Ihre Aufmerksamkeit."
- **Diskutieren (B2+):** "Ich sehe das etwas anders, weil …", "Da haben Sie/hast du einerseits recht, andererseits …", "Könnten wir uns darauf einigen, dass …?"
