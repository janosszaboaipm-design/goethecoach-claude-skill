# L1 Arabic (العربية — incl. Syrian and Egyptian dialects) → German interference patterns

Explanations follow the chat-language rule in SKILL.md; the L1 contrasts below apply either way. Match the learner's variety (MSA vs. dialect) when you give examples.

| Code | Pattern | Why (AR contrast) | Wrong → Correct |
|---|---|---|---|
| COPULA | *sein* missing in the present | AR nominal sentences have no copula (*أنا طالب*) | Ich Student. → Ich **bin** Student. |
| ARTICLE-INDEF | Indefinite article missing | AR marks only definiteness (*ال*). The indefinite is unmarked or shown by tanwīn | Ich habe Frage. → Ich habe **eine** Frage. |
| GENDER | Neuter missing, AR gender transferred | AR has only m/f | *شمس* (f) → die Sonne ✓ · *كتاب* (m) → der Buch ✗ → **das** Buch |
| V2 | VSO/SVO transfer | AR often puts the verb first or uses SVO after an adverb | Heute ich arbeite. → Heute **arbeite ich**. |
| SUBORD | Verb-final order not applied | not present in AR | …, weil ich habe Termin. → …, weil ich einen Termin **habe**. |
| RESUMPTIVE | Extra pronoun in a relative clause | AR repeats the pronoun (*الرجل الذي رأيته*) | der Mann, den ich **ihn** gesehen habe → der Mann, den ich gesehen habe |
| CHAIN | Long *und*-chains | AR text style links clauses with *و* | … und … und … und … → split the sentences and use *deshalb, danach, außerdem* |
| PREP | Preposition transfer | *في / على / ب* map to different German prepositions | Ich warte für dich. → Ich warte **auf** dich. |
| SPELL-PB | p/b and f/v confusion | there is no /p/ in Arabic | *Broblem* → **Problem**, *Birne* ↔ *Pirne* |
| SPELL-VOWEL | e/i and o/u confusion, umlauts | AR has 3 vowel qualities | *Minsch* → **Mensch**, *Tur* → **Tür** |
| CAPS | Capitalisation | the Arabic script has no capital letters, so both nouns and sentence starts are affected | |
| PUNCT | Comma use | the Arabic comma ، is used differently, and the German comma before subordinate clauses is missing | Ich glaube dass … → Ich glaube**,** dass … |

**Teaching tips for AR learners:**
- Build a "Satzbauplan" with position 1 / 2 / end.
- Write out every short sentence with "ist/bin/sind" explicitly.
- Spelling drill: minimal pairs such as p/b and e/i. The written exam penalises spelling that blocks meaning.
