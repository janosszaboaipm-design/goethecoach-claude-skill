# L1 Ukrainian (українська) → German interference patterns

Explanations follow the chat-language rule in SKILL.md; the L1 contrasts below apply either way. Many points also apply to Russian speakers.

| Code | Pattern | Why (UK contrast) | Wrong → Correct |
|---|---|---|---|
| V2 | Free word order breaks V2 | word order in UK is flexible (topic–comment) | Gestern ich war im Kino. → Gestern **war ich** im Kino. |
| SUBORD | Verb not final in a subordinate clause | UK has no verb-final rule | …, dass er kommt morgen. → …, dass er morgen **kommt**. |
| ARTICLE | Articles missing | UK has no articles | Ich habe Frage. → Ich habe **eine** Frage. |
| COPULA | *sein* missing in the present | UK usually drops *бути* in the present | Mein Bruder Arzt. → Mein Bruder **ist** Arzt. |
| GENDER | Gender transferred from UK | UK also has 3 genders, but the assignment differs | *сонце* (n) → das Sonne ✗ → **die** Sonne · *стіл* (m) → der Tisch ✓ |
| CASE-PREP | Preposition + case transferred | there are 6–7 cases in UK, but different government | Ich warte dich. → Ich warte **auf dich**. · Ich gratuliere dich. → Ich gratuliere **dir**. |
| REFLEXIVE | *-ся* mapped to *sich* | UK *-ся* verbs are not always reflexive in German, and vice versa | Ich lerne mich Deutsch. → Ich lerne Deutsch. · Ich erinnere das. → Ich erinnere **mich daran**. |
| TENSE | Aspect vs. tense | UK aspect (perfective/imperfective) is not the same as Perfekt/Präteritum | Ich habe gestern zwei Stunden gelesen ✓ — but aspect meaning is lost; teach "schon / gerade / noch nicht" |
| NEG | Double negation | *Я нічого не маю* | Ich habe nicht nichts. → Ich habe **nichts**. |
| PERFEKT-AUX | haben/sein with movement verbs | UK has no auxiliary for the past tense | Ich habe nach Hause gegangen. → Ich **bin** nach Hause gegangen. |
| CAPS | Nouns in lowercase | UK capitalises only proper nouns | |
| SPELL | Transliteration and sounds | h/g (*г*), ch, ü/ö, ie/i | Hund → *Gund*, zwölf → *zwelf* |

**Teaching tips for UK learners:**
- Strength: case thinking already exists. Map cases explicitly: Akk ≈ знахідний, Dat ≈ давальний.
- The main work is V2, verb-final order, and articles.
