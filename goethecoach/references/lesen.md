# Lesen — generating and scoring reading practice

## Generating a Teil

1. Choose the Teil type from `exam-formats.md` for the level.
2. Write **original** texts in the matching genre: blog post, newspaper article, advertisements, forum comments, house rules, or information board. Text length should fit the level:
   - A1: 30–60 words per text
   - A2: 60–150 words
   - B1: 150–350 words
   - B2: 300–500 words
   - C1/C2: 500–800 words
3. Write the items (usually 5–7 per Teil):
   - **richtig/falsch:** paraphrase the statements. Never copy a sentence word for word from the text.
   - **Multiple choice (a/b/c):** exactly one option is correct. The distractors should be plausible and use words from the text (the typical Goethe trap).
   - **Matching (ads/texts to people):** include at least one person with no matching ad ("0"/"x"), if the format has that option.
   - **Gap texts (B2+):** the removed sentences must fit only one gap by content and by the pronouns/connectors they contain.
4. Put the answer key in a **separate message**, or keep it hidden until the learner answers. Never show it together with the task.
5. Give the time guideline (e.g. "ca. 12 Minuten für diesen Teil").

## Scoring

- One point per item, unless the format weights items differently. The module score is the % correct across all Teile done. The pass line is 60 %.
- For each wrong answer, explain in the learner's language:
  - the exact sentence in the text that decides the answer (quote it)
  - why the tempting distractor is wrong: synonym trap, negation, a word from the text in a different context, or an extreme word like *immer/nie/nur*
- Afterwards, name the pattern behind the wrong answers ("Du übersiehst Negationen", "Du wählst die Option mit denselben Wörtern wie im Text") and give 2–3 strategy tips:
  - read the items first
  - underline key words
  - mark synonyms
  - don't get stuck on unknown words

## Vocabulary follow-up

Offer a short word list with 8–12 words from the text. Include the article and plural for nouns, and for verbs the Perfekt form plus the preposition they take. Then offer a mini-quiz in the tutoring phase.
