# Goethe-Zertifikat — exam formats (Lesen, Schreiben, Sprechen)

Use this to generate exam-authentic tasks. Formats follow the current Goethe-Zertifikat exams for adults. Details (exact item counts, minutes) can change — if a learner quotes a different official figure, trust the current Modellsatz on goethe.de and say so.

General conventions for every task you write:
- Header: `Goethe-Zertifikat <Level> · <Modul> · Teil <n>` + time and target length.
- Situation in 1–3 sentences, instruction, then the Leitpunkte as a bulleted list.
- Register is explicit: informal (du), semi-formal, formal (Sie).
- Topics come from everyday life at A1–A2 (Familie, Wohnen, Einkaufen, Freizeit, Arbeit), social and working life at B1–B2 (Umwelt, Medien, Arbeit, Bildung, Gesundheit, Wohnen in der Stadt) and abstract/academic topics at C1–C2 (Wissenschaft, Gesellschaft, Arbeitswelt der Zukunft, Sprache, Kultur).

## A1 — Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | ~25 min | 3 Teile: short messages/emails (richtig/falsch), two ads per item (a/b choice), signs and notices (richtig/falsch) |
| Schreiben | ~20 min | Teil 1: fill in a form with personal data from a short text. Teil 2: short message **~30 words**, 3 Leitpunkte, register given |
| Sprechen | ~15 min, group | Teil 1: introduce yourself (Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby) + spell/give a number. Teil 2: ask and answer questions on a topic using word cards. Teil 3: make a request and react, using picture cards |

## A2 — Goethe-Zertifikat A2

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | ~30 min | 4 Teile: newspaper article (MC), information board / directory (MC), email (MC), ads matched to people |
| Schreiben | ~30 min | Teil 1: SMS/short message **~20–30 words**, 3 Leitpunkte. Teil 2: email **~30–40 words**, 3 Leitpunkte, often semi-formal/formal |
| Sprechen | ~15 min, pair | Teil 1: questions about the person using word cards. Teil 2: talk about your life (card with a question + 4 prompts). Teil 3: plan something together with two diaries (find a time) |

## B1 — Goethe-Zertifikat B1 (modular, each module 100 points, pass 60)

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min | 5 Teile: blog/personal text (richtig/falsch), press articles (MC), ads matched to situations, opinions in comments (ja/nein about a position), rules/instructions (MC) |
| Schreiben | 60 min | Teil 1: informal email **~80 words**, 3 Leitpunkte (40 P). Teil 2: forum opinion **~80 words** reacting to a statement (40 P). Teil 3: short formal message **~40 words**, e.g. apologise/decline to a teacher, boss (20 P) |
| Sprechen | ~15 min, pair, 15 min prep | Teil 1: plan something together (~3 min). Teil 2: present a topic (~3 min) along 5 slides: introduce topic, personal experience, situation in home country, pros/cons + own opinion, conclusion + thanks. Teil 3: react to the partner's presentation (feedback + one question) and answer the examiner's/partner's question |

## B2 — Goethe-Zertifikat B2 (modular)

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min | 5 Teile: opinions matched to statements, article with MC, text with gaps for removed sentences, statements vs opinions, rules/regulations matched to headings |
| Schreiben | 75 min | Teil 1: forum post expressing an opinion **~150 words**, 4 Leitpunkte, choose 1 of 2 topics (~50 min). Teil 2: formal message to a superior/institution **~100 words**, e.g. apology, request, complaint (~25 min) |
| Sprechen | ~15 min, pair, 15 min prep | Teil 1: talk (~4 min) on 1 of 2 topics: present options, explain one in detail, pros/cons, own opinion; then answer the partner's question. Teil 2: discussion (~5 min) on a controversial question, exchange arguments, react, reach a position |

## C1 — Goethe-Zertifikat C1 (modular)

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 65 min | 4 Teile incl. a longer text with gaps, MC on an academic/journalistic text, matching statements, detail comprehension |
| Schreiben | 75 min | Teil 1: forum/opinion piece **~230 words** on a social topic, with given aspects (weighing arguments, own position, examples). Teil 2: formal email/letter **~120 words** in a professional context |
| Sprechen | ~15–20 min, pair | Teil 1: presentation (~3–4 min) with structure and examples. Teil 2: discussion — argue, counter, negotiate a solution |

## C2 — Goethe-Zertifikat C2: Großes Deutsches Sprachdiplom

| Module | Time | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Lesen | 80 min | several Teile with complex, partly literary/academic texts: gaps, MC, matching, detail and inference |
| Schreiben | 80 min | Teil 1: extended text (essay/article/commentary) **at least ~350 words** from a choice of topics. Teil 2: stylistic reworking of a text (register, precision, cohesion) |
| Sprechen | ~15 min | Teil 1: presentation (~4 min) + follow-up questions. Teil 2: discussion with nuanced argumentation |

## Task templates

**Schreiben task (all levels):**
```
Goethe-Zertifikat B1 · Schreiben · Teil 1 · ca. 20 Minuten · ca. 80 Wörter

Ihr Freund Jonas hat Sie zu seiner Geburtstagsparty eingeladen, aber Sie
können an dem Tag nicht kommen.

Schreiben Sie Jonas eine E-Mail:
• Bedanken Sie sich für die Einladung.
• Erklären Sie, warum Sie nicht kommen können.
• Machen Sie einen Vorschlag für ein anderes Treffen.

Schreiben Sie eine E-Mail (circa 80 Wörter). Denken Sie an Anrede und Gruß.
```

**Sprechen task:** give the learner the full task card, the preparation time, and your role (Prüfer/in and/or Partner/in). See `rubric-sprechen.md` for the simulation protocol.

**Lesen task:** see `lesen.md`.
