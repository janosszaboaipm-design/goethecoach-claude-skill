# Study plan — from diagnosis to exam day

## 1. Diagnose (max 3 questions, ask only what you don't know yet)
- Target level and exam date. For B1 and above: which modules they will take.
- Time available per week.
- Their weakest module according to them, or according to your evaluations this session.

If there is no evaluation yet, offer a short diagnostic: one short Schreiben task and one Lesen Teil. Estimates without evidence are guesses. Still give a **provisional plan** right away, labelled as based on self-assessment. Adjust it after the first evaluation instead of blocking on the diagnostic.

Hören is not covered by this skill. If the learner takes the Hören module, include it in the plan with official Modellsätze and German podcasts or radio as self-study.

## 2. Prioritise by points per hour
1. Modules below 60 % come first. Modules above 70 % only get maintenance.
2. Inside Schreiben, the fastest wins are:
   - Leitpunkte coverage
   - register, greeting and closing
   - length
   - a paragraph template
   - the top 2 error patterns
3. Inside Sprechen: redemittel for the task types, and a presentation structure the learner can reproduce under stress.
4. Inside Lesen: strategy and time before vocabulary.

## 3. Plan format
Weekly table up to the exam, realistic for the stated hours:

```
Woche | Fokus | Aufgaben (konkret) | Ziel/Check
1     | Schreiben Teil 1 + Verbposition | 3 E-Mails, Drill V2 ×2 | ≥ 60 % bei Teil 1
2     | Sprechen Teil 2 Präsentation    | 2 Themen üben, Redemittel-Liste | 3 Min. frei sprechen
…
Letzte Woche | Modellsatz unter Prüfungsbedingungen, nur Wiederholung, keine neuen Themen
```

- Include one **full mock exam** under exam conditions, 2–3 weeks before the date.
- If the date is less than 2 weeks away, give an emergency plan: templates, redemittel, time management, 2 mock tasks per module.
- For self-scheduling, recommend fixed short sessions (e.g. 4 × 30 min) over rare long ones.

## 4. Follow-up
End the plan with a clear next action for today, e.g. "Heute: Schreiben B1 Teil 1, soll ich dir eine Aufgabe geben?". Mention the app dashboard for tracking (see `product-bridge.md`, trigger 3).
